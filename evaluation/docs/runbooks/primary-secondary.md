# Primary and secondary model stages

Primary entry requires an independently authorized primary entry bundle linked to an accepted pilot exit. The sealed plan contains exactly 32 tasks: four in each F1-F8 family. Its fixed complete-ITT envelope is 512 opaque units: every task is assigned to M0 in two arm slots over eight repeats. Attempts, failures, unavailable fields, exposure, and costs remain assigned units; neither replacements nor enrollment expansion are permitted.

Close primary only after every one of the 512 units is terminal and reconciliation is complete. Preserve the immutable receipts for simultaneous intervals, harm tails, Pareto surface, panel, safety, analysis, and every explicit frozen claim decision. A categorical decision remains blocked for its affected family. A frozen power result below 0.80 remains estimation-only. Neither result is changed by later secondary evidence.

Secondary is optional. After a reconciled, accepted primary exit, separately authorize one secondary stage for each currently qualified M1 or M2 role. Use the fixed balanced 16-task subset, two arm slots, and three repeats: exactly 96 units for the role. Each role has a distinct stage, protocol, entry bundle, assignments, analysis, evidence, report, and claim stratum. Record unavailable or drifted roles as unavailable; do not substitute another model. Two qualified roles produce 192 units, which is the maximum.

For a paused model stage, validate the unchanged locks, receipts, and model pin before resuming. Resume only units that have never started. If the model is unavailable or drifted, pause starts and retain the evidence already produced.

Primary limits are sealed at 128M task-agent tokens, 24M framework input tokens, 8M framework output tokens, ten elapsed days, and concurrency four, alongside independently supplied AI-credit, USD, and task-class active-time caps. Secondary limits are sealed at 48M, 9M, and 3M respectively across five additional days. M1 and M2 each retain a 24M/4.5M/1.5M subcap; an unavailable role cannot transfer that allocation.
